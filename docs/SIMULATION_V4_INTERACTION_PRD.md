# V4 — manipulate the model, not a parameter panel

20 September 2026. V3 made the diagrams functional, but the user rejected slider-led control as an unconvincing experience. V4 changes the interaction grammar across every teaching figure.

## Experience contract

A person should act on an object in the model and follow its consequences. A signal can be launched. A profile can be followed. A barrier can be removed. Two traits can be connected. A shock can be placed in time. An announcement can be sent to a stakeholder. The resulting motion should explain which state changed and why. Controls must not merely recolor a picture or replace a caption.

The visual language remains restrained: neutral surfaces, legible sans-serif type, a small number of blue/gray scientific states. Avoid confetti, decorative glow, fake loading, gratuitous camera movement and unsupported claims about live inference. Numerical parameters are secondary inspection, inside closed details where still useful.

## Overview

Keep the 96 stable profiles and four-stage account. Select a distribution source and launch a signal; animate its traversal through the same population. Select any profile to follow its exposure, memory, decision and resource contribution across stages. In memory, play elapsed time or reinforce the signal. In choice, remove or restore named friction barriers and watch people cross the action boundary. In allocation, transfer capacity to a group while preserving the choice count. Offer a deliberate play-through that carries the signal through the four stages, with a visible stop control and immediate response to manual input.

The distribution-error figure should let readers transfer probability mass between outcomes directly, rather than hiding the distribution behind a generic shift slider. Preserve normalization and compute the resulting error.

## Five use cases

1. Product: remove setup/time/switching obstacles along the path to trial.
2. Marketing: select a message card and launch a sequential exposure, recall and action wave.
3. Segmentation: connect trait nodes and watch their joint population rearrange while each margin stays fixed.
4. Scenarios: place a shock on the timeline and follow attention, beliefs and choice at different speeds.
5. Communications: select a stakeholder, send a particular announcement and trace its interpretation and response.

The15 authored finding diagrams retain their direct inspection controls; they are research outputs, not model-parameter sliders.

## Research figures

Directly manipulate a belief marker against bid/ask; place exposure pulses and play a memory trajectory; transfer joint-population mass between cells while maintaining the one-way margins. Preserve article arguments, sources and explicit teaching status.

## Acceptance

No primary range-slider presentation remains in the teaching experience. Every gesture has a keyboard/touch equivalent, a visible affordance and a named consequence. Point identity and evidence units remain stable through transitions. Animation stops on manual interruption, offscreen/document-hidden states and reduced-motion preferences. Check continuous interaction sequences, not only screenshots; test responsive sizes and the exact local artifact before updating port4282.
