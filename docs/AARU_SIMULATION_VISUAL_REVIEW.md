## Translation into Backer

1. Keep Backer's graphite, ivory, warm amber, and mint rather than copying Aaru's white product shell or blue controls.
2. Define a decision, a population and a comparison before exposing results. Fifteen fictional case dossiers preserve that context while the browser model recalculates.
3. Keep scientific explanation next to working instruments: finite attention budget, conditional decision funnel, dependency tree, distribution error, cohort comparison and full time trajectory.
4. Make generated plates explorable through semantic HTML controls. Numbers, definitions and state remain live HTML/SVG; generated imagery makes no numerical claim.
5. Retain a visible question, selected day, baseline, alternative, units and assumptions in the console. CSV exports the actual calculated series.
6. Do not adopt Aaru's claims about trained global populations, licensed data, individual training, or customer validations as Backer claims. The shipped model is an educational conditional simulation with synthetic cohorts and assumed parameters.
7. Match progressive disclosure and editorial spacing. Keep all controls keyboard-operable, responsive, and usable with reduced motion.

The reference audit records observed rendered behavior; it is not an endorsement or independent validation of Aaru's scientific claims.
