# Backer social banner

Asset: `img/backer-social-20261002.png` (3840 × 2016 PNG).

Composed with the approved amber logo, preserved as 3840 × 3840 lossless transparent artwork in `img/backer-mark.webp`. The versioned filename allows social crawlers to fetch a new image independently of any cached older asset.

Design: near-black charcoal, warm ivory type, and the approved amber mark with its atmospheric glow. All text remains within the central safe area for common social crops.

Composition: the approved mark appears beside “Backer AI” in the upper left and at a larger scale on the right. Large left-aligned headline on two lines: “Predict where” / “attention flows.” Smaller descriptor: “Simulation science. Better decisions.” The logo artwork is embedded directly, preserving its silhouette and material instead of recreating it.

The banner is shared across public pages and all existing language URLs. Individual pages retain their own social titles and descriptions. It is a brand illustration, not measured simulation output.
