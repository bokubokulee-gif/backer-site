A view of the future, recorded before it arrives.

Before the release reaches its deadline, the Lab records a forecast of whether it will reach 100K views. The target, time window, and outcome source are fixed in advance.

Market prices express beliefs. Independently measured outcomes test them. We will compare model forecasts, market forecasts, and their combination across repeated outcomes.
