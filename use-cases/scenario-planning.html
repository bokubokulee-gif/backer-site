Inspect the assumptions

The signal strength is fixed at 65. Let τ = max(0, day − event day). Attention = 18 + 0.72×65×exp(−τ/7)×(1−exp(−τ/0.55)); agreement = 22 + 0.23×65×(1−exp(−τ/3))×exp(−τ/30); add-position choice = 8 + 0.14×65×(1−exp(−τ/4))×exp(−τ/18). Before injection, the three shares remain at 18%, 22% and 8%. A shock at day 14 has no elapsed response time within this window. These are separate daily snapshots, without holdings, price, order-size or liquidity updates.
