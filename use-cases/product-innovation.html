Inspect the assumptions

Seventy-two of 100 synthetic profiles consider the product. Their readiness is evenly spaced between 0 and 1. The starting threshold is 0.12. Active obstacles add 0.20 for time, 0.25 for setup and 0.27 for switching. A profile advances when readiness exceeds the cumulative threshold. The remaining 28 profiles stay outside consideration. Trial is an immediate decision, not repeated use.

The model uses stated assumptions. The study below is a separate illustrative case.
