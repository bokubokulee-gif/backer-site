01 / The evidence

An early advantage.
An edge to renew.

Chen, Sialm and Xu document a substantial early advantage for disclosed AI hedge funds that diminished after 2017. Better models spread. Differentiated information becomes more valuable.

The Backer thesis

Knowing the business
is only part of the trade.

The same earnings release reaches investors with different positions, convictions and histories. Some add immediately. Others wait, reduce exposure or delegate under preset rules. Those differences shape order flow.

We are building the behavioral layer for systematic trading: combine observed trading histories with event context to model individual responses. The objective is a new source of alpha rooted in how people act.

05 / Building a durable research engine

Better models.
Deeper behavioral evidence.

Our foundation compounds through richer behavioral histories, Jev’s structured judgments and advances in foundation models. We measure progress in better forecasts of real decisions and incremental value after trading costs.

  1. Freeze the information set.

    Use consented or licensed histories. Record exposure, positions, timestamps and missing data before the event.

  2. Beat the relevant baselines.

    Hold out people, events and future periods. Compare public-data, price/volume, flow and no-history models. Score calibration and action timing.

  3. Measure value under real conditions.

    Replay spreads, fees, impact, queue position and missed fills. Test capacity, regimes and signal decay before prospective paper trading.

  4. Promote models on evidence.

    Pin versions. Admit a new Jev or foundation model only after it improves held-out outcomes within cost and latency limits.
