Inspect the assumptions

The same 100 synthetic profiles retain 50% high interest and 50% available time. Independent joint cells contain 25 / 25 / 25 / 25 people; connected cells contain 40 / 10 / 10 / 40. The connection is an assumed positive association, not a causal link. Cell trial probabilities stay at 72%, 12%, 18% and 4%; expected response is the weighted mean.

The model uses stated assumptions. The study below is a separate illustrative case.
