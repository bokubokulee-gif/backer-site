Prospective program

A chain of evidence, not one benchmark.

Every stage produces a reviewable record. A failed stage stops the claim from advancing; scale, realism, and polished agent dialogue cannot substitute for the missing evidence.

01

Future study

Sampling and consent

Not started

Recruit the people whose behavior the study intends to describe—including those who saw an opportunity and chose not to act.

Input

Active and infrequent traders, creator supporters, purchasers, scouts, lurkers, non-buyers, and non-adopters.

Procedure

Keep trader and web-audience recruitment frames separate. Preregister strata, recruitment channels, inclusion rules, exclusions, power analysis, compensation, retention, withdrawal, deletion, and controlled-access terms.

Advance only if

The achieved sample covers the declared target population, consent is comprehensible and revocable, and small cohorts have enough audit power.

Failure condition

Convenience recruitment, actor-only sampling, inferred sensitive traits, or a fixed target of 5,000 chosen to match the demo.

Record produced Recruitment, consent, and withdrawal ledger

02

Human grounding

Interview collection

Future phase

Use broad semi-structured interviews to ground context without placing the exact benchmark answers inside the interview.

Input

Consented voice or text interviews, post-interview surveys, behavioral tasks, forecast diaries, and narrowly scoped opt-in histories.

Procedure

Compare an approximately two-hour condition with shorter ablations. Preserve question provenance, interviewer version, transcript edits, and participant review.

Advance only if

Interview-conditioned agents improve held-out decisions or distributions beyond demographic, persona, survey-only, and simple statistical baselines.

Failure condition

Benchmark leakage, unreviewable sensitive inferences, or model-generated reflections presented as participant testimony.

Record produced Versioned, access-controlled interview corpus and ablation report

03

Human noise ceiling

Repeat measures

Future phase

Measure each participant against their own later response before interpreting model error as stable human behavior.

Input

The same core choices, confidence judgments, and attention tasks repeated after a fixed delay.

Procedure

Begin with a two-week retest as a design reference. Use longer follow-ups separately to distinguish inconsistency from genuine preference change.

Advance only if

Model performance is reported both raw and relative to measured human self-consistency, with uncertainty around the comparison.

Failure condition

A single measurement is treated as ground truth, or retest disagreement is silently counted as model failure.

Record produced Retest reliability and raw-versus-normalized performance report

04

Backer addition

Prospective holdouts

Not preregistered

Freeze the information set and prediction before a genuinely unseen future is allowed to resolve.

Input

Unseen people, markets, creators, topics, campaigns, platforms, time windows, and exogenous shocks.

Procedure

Timestamp the model version, source cutoff, exclusions, forecast distribution, horizon, endpoints, abstentions, and failure conditions at t0.

Advance only if

Whole-event and out-of-time holdouts beat persistence, recent-trend, market-only, and non-agent baselines after resolution.

Failure condition

Random-row splits from the same episode, post-outcome tuning, unlogged source updates, or selective publication.

Record produced Timestamped forecast registry, including misses and abstentions

05

Causal phase

Randomized intervention tests

Future phase

A model can forecast an outcome and still be wrong about how to change it. Intervention effects require their own test.

Input

Consented, low-risk experiments on information quality, recommendation diversity, creator response, social-proof visibility, timing, or action friction.

Procedure

Preregister the human randomization unit, assignment, spillover assumptions, primary estimand, sample size, and acceptance tolerances. Hold the simulated population and seed constant; predict effect direction and magnitude before opening the human result.

Advance only if

Observed average and heterogeneous treatment effects agree with the preregistered simulated estimates within declared tolerances.

Failure condition

The same model invents the treatment, simulates it, and judges its own success—or observational self-selection is described as causality.

Record produced Randomization log and preregistered treatment-effect report

06

Uncertainty discipline

Calibration

Pending outcomes

A useful system must know how often its confidence is warranted, not only how often its top choice is right.

Input

Frozen probability forecasts, numeric trajectories, prediction intervals, abstentions, treatment-effect forecasts, and resolved outcomes.

Procedure

Use Brier and log score for events; MAE, RMSE, or CRPS for trajectories; reliability curves, ECE, and interval coverage for uncertainty.

Advance only if

Ordinary forecasts and intervention forecasts are separately calibrated, stable across time, and incrementally better than simpler baselines.

Failure condition

One accuracy number hides distribution collapse, confidence inflation, poor interval coverage, or a weak baseline.

Record produced Calibration curves, proper scores, coverage, and baseline comparisons

07

Failure distribution

Subgroup audits

Pending study

Aggregate performance is incomplete if the model is systematically uncertain or wrong for specific cohorts.

Input

Consented cohort attributes, predefined intersections, missingness, sample sizes, errors, calibration, coverage, abstention, and treatment effects.

Procedure

Report gaps with confidence intervals and low-power warnings. Audit inferred/synthetic fields separately from participant-provided attributes.

Advance only if

Material gaps are bounded, published, and governed by correction, restricted-use, or retirement thresholds.

Failure condition

A parity statistic is presented as proof of fairness, small cells are hidden, or sensitive attributes are scraped or guessed.

Record produced Disclosure-safe subgroup audit and restrictions on use

Proposed collection routes

More ways to observe behavior—without pretending a click is a person.

Each route is optional, consented where person-linked, minimized to the forecast question, and versioned by evidence strength.
