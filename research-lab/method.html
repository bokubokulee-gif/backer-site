Trading behavior / Method

From attention to a priced decision.

Specify what a person notices, what they believe, and what they can execute. Each is a different object of inference.

Backer Research20 September 2026Research note · 01

Research protocol · The preview illustrates scenarios and market context. Prospective trading performance has not been evaluated.

01

Define the event before modeling the trader.

The unit of analysis is a person–contract–time observation. A binary outcome Y refers to a particular contract’s written resolution rule, source and deadline. An agent’s belief q is its probability for that outcome given information available at the forecast timestamp.

A last trade, a bid, an ask and a probability estimate cannot be substituted for one another. Kalshi’s order-book documentation exposes resting prices and quantities; an indicative midpoint need not be executable. Its settlement documentation also separates closing from determination. Outcome labels must follow the relevant contract, including exceptional provisions.

02

Separate selection, belief and execution.

A candidate mechanism first assigns scarce attention across available contracts. Exposure and prior interests determine which contracts enter consideration. Evidence updates a conditional belief; existing positions, transaction costs and available depth constrain the resulting order.

exposure → consideration → belief → order → fillThese are proposed measurement stages, not five outputs already estimated by the public preview.

An observed buy therefore leaves several explanations open: the trader noticed a new contract, revised a belief, changed a hedge, or faced a different quote. A behavioral model should predict the action distribution conditional on these states rather than relabel attention as demand.

03

Fix the forecast before the outcome.

The proposed evaluation records the contract definition, timestamp, information cutoff and horizon before the result is known. All observations from the same underlying event stay in one split. Holding out random price ticks would leak nearly identical events across training and evaluation.

BS = (1 / N) Σᵢ (qᵢ − yᵢ)²Brier score for N binary forecasts; lower is better. A probability forecast and its settled binary outcome use the same event definition.

Compare against a base rate and a contemporaneous market benchmark at the same horizon. Report calibration by probability bin, category, time to event and liquidity, alongside sample counts. Proper scores assess probabilities; threshold accuracy discards how confidently the forecast was made. Gneiting and Raftery give the scoring-rule foundation.

Kalshi’s calibration study is a useful protocol reference because it examines horizon and participation. Its exchange-level results do not validate a Backer model. Near-resolution performance also answers a different question from forecasting before the relevant information becomes public.

04

Evaluate actions separately from outcomes.

Trade prediction requires timestamped action labels: eligible traders who buy, sell or do neither within a declared interval. Evaluate held-out action probabilities and calibration separately from event probabilities. Executed volume, turnover and account balances are different targets again.

A strategy evaluation would additionally require fees, spread, slippage, rejected orders, position limits and a specified sizing policy. Positive probability skill alone establishes none of those results. The present preview illustrates hypotheses about attention and action; a reproducible prospective test remains the next evidentiary step.
