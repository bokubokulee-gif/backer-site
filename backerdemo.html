Attention needs an evidence trail.

Backer keeps the source, observation time, metric meaning, and access state attached to every fact.

SOURCE-NATIVE evidence, not a universal score

Public creator records are for research and watchlists only. Discovery evidence never creates consent, tradability, or a real-money market.

Records Every creator, identity, work, and native source record stays distinct and traceable.
Signals Reach, traction, momentum, coverage, and confidence remain separate evidence dimensions.
Limits Unavailable or permission-gated facts stay unavailable. Backer does not turn missing evidence into zero.

Support already follows people. Backer makes the evidence inspectable.

Tips, memberships, subscriptions, and public engagement all describe different relationships. Backer keeps those meanings separate.

Direct support

Tips, memberships, subscriptions, and purchases are support signals—not promises of financial return.

Public evidence

Source-native metrics, timestamps, methods, freshness, and confidence remain reviewable.

Creator graph

Platform identities link only through reviewed evidence—never because two people share a name.

You can't invest in a market where the metrics can't be trusted.

Every number on an internet profile can be fabricated. Followers, views, likes, comments — all purchasable.
An entire shadow industry exists to inflate them, and platforms have little incentive to stop it.

Are these followers real humans or bots?

Did this video get 1 million views in a day organically, or bought overnight?

Are these comment section for real? These profiles look oddly new.

How is the viewer retention — do they come back — or one-time exposures?

Without data verifications, investment isn't investment. It's gambling.
