The infrastructure problem

You can't invest in a market where the metrics can't be trusted.

Every number on a creator's profile is potentially fabricated. Followers, views, likes, comments — all purchasable. An entire shadow industry exists to inflate them, and platforms have little incentive to stop it.

Are these 4,000 followers real humans or purchased bots?

Did this video get 80K views organically, or bought overnight?

Is engagement genuine, or manufactured by coordinated rings?

Are these fans sticky — do they come back — or one-time exposures?

Without data verifications, investment isn't investment. It's gambling.
